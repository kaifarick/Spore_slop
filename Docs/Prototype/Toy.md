\# Prototype 0 — Toy



\## Purpose



Prototype 0 is a physical cooperative gameplay sandbox.



The goal is to discover whether simple physical interactions between players, objects and NPCs can produce fun emergent situations.



This prototype does not attempt to represent the final game.



\---



\# Core Player Actions



The player should eventually be able to:



1\. Run

2\. Jump

3\. Fall

4\. Grab

5\. Carry

6\. Drag

7\. Release

8\. Eat

9\. Attack

10\. Call / make a vocal sound

11\. Interact with objects

12\. Interact with NPCs

13\. Enter ragdoll

14\. Recover from ragdoll



\---



\# Physical Entities



The interaction system should eventually support:



\- generic objects

\- food

\- resources

\- corpses

\- injured players

\- babies / young creatures

\- NPCs

\- large/heavy objects



\---



\# Cooperative Interactions



Potential interactions:



\- rescue an injured player

\- carry an injured player

\- drag an injured player

\- move a corpse

\- carry resources

\- move large objects together

\- help another player recover

\- manipulate the same object together



The exact mechanics are not finalized.



\---



\# NPC



NPCs are currently only experimental.



Initial interaction:



Player approaches NPC

→ interaction becomes available

→ player initiates mating interaction

→ prototype confirms the interaction.



The reproduction system itself is not yet designed.



\---



\# Ragdoll



Ragdoll should support testing:



\- falling

\- collisions

\- impacts

\- being knocked down

\- accidental situations

\- rescue

\- carrying

\- dragging

\- recovery



The final health/injury/death model is not defined yet.



\---



\# Prototype Environment



The environment should be extremely small.



It should contain:



\- flat ground

\- slopes

\- platforms

\- obstacles

\- gaps

\- simple physics objects

\- food

\- resources

\- one or more NPCs

\- a few objects requiring interaction



No final art is required.



\---



\# Questions We Are Testing



\## Movement



Does the player feel good to control?



\## Physics



Does physical interaction produce fun situations?



\## Carrying



Is carrying another entity interesting?



\## Dragging



Does dragging create useful gameplay and funny situations?



\## Ragdoll



Does falling / ragdolling feel satisfying rather than frustrating?



\## Cooperation



Do players naturally help each other?



\## Emergent gameplay



Do unexpected interactions create memorable situations?



\## NPC interaction



Does interacting with NPCs feel natural enough to become a foundation for future mechanics?
